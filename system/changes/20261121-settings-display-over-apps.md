Summary: Choose which apps draw over other apps
Details: Each app's page in Settings shows whether it may display over other apps, add a panel, draw the desktop background or take the keyboard, and when it last did. Apps › Display over other apps lists the apps allowed to draw above games and full-screen apps, and those that asked and were turned down, each with a switch.
