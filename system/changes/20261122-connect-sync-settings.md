Summary: Sync in Settings
Details: Suno account › Sync turns Sync on with a recovery key, chooses what syncs, lists app data and devices, and joins a new device by code or recovery key. The device joining compares its code with the one on your other device, and stops if they don't match. After a device is removed, Sync shows the new recovery key until you save it.
