Summary: Continue where you left off on your other computer
Details: Close one computer and open another: after you unlock it, Luma asks "Continue from ThinkPad?" and opens the same apps with their windows in the same places, the same one in front. What's open on the second computer stays; Continue opens on a new desktop. Your windows travel end-to-end encrypted and only to your own computers and phones, never to a TV.
