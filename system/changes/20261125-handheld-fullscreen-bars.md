Summary: Fullscreen games and videos get the whole screen in Tablet and Phone modes
Details: An app that goes fullscreen, such as a game or a video player, now hides the status bar and the home bar in Tablet and Phone modes, as it hides the top bar on Desktop. Swipe down from the top edge to see the status bar for a few seconds. The bars come back when the app leaves fullscreen.
