Summary: Approve new devices and see sync status
Details: A device asking to join Sync shows its code on your other devices, with Not me and Approve. The status island shows Sync only while it is syncing, paused or needs attention. Apps still restoring from your other devices open in Valet. The card appears before the code does and waits for it, and a new recovery key shows in the status island.
