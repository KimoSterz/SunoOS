Summary: See and stop apps drawing over other apps
Details: A violet mark in the status area shows the icon of every app drawing over other apps; clicking it lists them, each with Stop. An app asking for one of these abilities gets a notification from Settings with Allow and Not now, asked once. Luma's own installed apps are named correctly in these lists and in Settings.
