Summary: One permissions page for every app
Details: Settings › Apps shows the same page for every app, whatever its format: its files, devices, network, background and what it used recently, including what it was refused. A new list shows the apps that can use all your files. Removing an app names the documents it kept and can move them to Documents. When an open app takes a change only the next time it opens, the page says so and can restart it. When an app is refused a folder, a notification asks once whether to allow it.
