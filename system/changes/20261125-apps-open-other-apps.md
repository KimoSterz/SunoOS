Summary: Apps can no longer open other apps without you
Details: An app can open a link or a file in your default app for it, or in another Luma app (any file except programs and installers), and the app that opens it gets permission to read it. Opening it in any other app, or in an app added to your own applications folder, asks you first in Open With. Apps can no longer pass programs to another app, and installers, app packages, torrents and documents with macros always ask.
