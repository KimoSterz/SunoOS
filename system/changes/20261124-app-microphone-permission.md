Summary: Recording now follows each app's microphone permission
Details: Every installed app gets a sound connection of its own. It can play sound as before, but it can only record when it has the microphone permission, and turning that off in Settings stops a recording at once. Apps can no longer change the computer's sound setup or reach other apps' sound through that connection. Call, chat and recording apps installed earlier keep the microphone.
